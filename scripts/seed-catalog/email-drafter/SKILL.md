# Email Drafter

Draft clear, on-brand emails from a short prompt.

## When to use
When the user asks to write, reply to, or rework an email.

## How to draft
1. Ask for the recipient, goal, and tone if they aren't clear.
2. Keep it concise — lead with the ask, then the context.
3. Offer a subject line and a short + a longer variant when useful.
